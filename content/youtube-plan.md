# Mashnu AI: YouTube + Instagram plan (video-first)

## Channel concept
"Building an AI company from India in public." Screen-recorded builds, real code, real numbers. One long video per week, 3 Shorts/Reels per week cut from it. Same clip posts to YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

Why this works for revenue: SMB owners and international clients find you through demos they can watch. Every long video ends on one offer: the AI lead responder pilot.

## Weekly rhythm
- Mon: record the long video (screen share + voice). Build what you're showing, don't fake it.
- Tue-Wed: cut 3 Shorts from it. Engine generates scripts: POST /content/generate with platforms ["youtube","short"].
- Thu: publish long video. Fri/Sat/Sun: one Short per day on YouTube + Reels.
- Sun: pull view/retention numbers, note what hooked. Feed back into the next scripts.

## First 4 long videos (all demonstrable today, nothing invented)
1. **I built an AI content engine in one afternoon** (FastAPI + LangGraph + free Gemini API). Demo: note in, drafts out, approval on Telegram. Proof moment: live generation.
2. **Zapier vs building your own: what happens when every step calls an LLM.** Prototype vs own the core. Show the architecture diagram.
3. **WhatsApp AI lead responder for clinics: build part 1.** The Business Cloud API, webhook, qualification flow.
4. **Part 2: connect it to a CRM and show the dashboard.** Ends with the pilot offer.

## Rules so it stays honest and grows
- Show real screens. Never claim clients, revenue, or results you don't have.
- Blur API keys and tokens on screen.
- Hook in the first 2 seconds, captions always on, one idea per Short.
- Same hook structure as your X posts: concrete number or demo, then verdict.

## 4. LinkedIn (lesson)
Lesson from designing our AI content pipeline: the model is not the hard part.
The hard part is specifics. Generic input gives generic posts.
So the pipeline pulls from real founder notes (numbers, failures, decisions) and rejects drafts that have nothing concrete.
If your AI content sounds like everyone else's, check the input before blaming the model.
